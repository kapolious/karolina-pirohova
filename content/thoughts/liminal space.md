although my algorithms seem to think so, i haven't seen the movie Backrooms yet. i have not really looked up the plot, but what i have noticed is the absurd overuse of the word liminal. office spaces, bus stations, hotel rooms. all of those seem to be the example of a spacial middle stage. transitional in between.  

but that is not my kind of liminal space, or at least i do not give those spaces such value. liminal space is the place in woods where two tree trunks touch exactly two times. one for the future and one for the past. a portal. is unexpected, not mundane.
